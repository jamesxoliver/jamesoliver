# Renewable Energy Is Nuclear

A wheel turns under falling water. The water fell as rain, the rain rose as vapor and the vapor was lifted by sunlight. The question is where the energy of each source of power comes from when it is traced to its start.

Nearly all of it comes from the fusion of hydrogen in the Sun. Sunlight is that energy eight minutes after it leaves the Sun. Wind is the same energy days later, falling water weeks to years later, wood years to decades later and coal hundreds of millions of years later. The heat within the Earth comes in part from the decay of heavy nuclei, which is also nuclear. Only the tides and the heat left from the Earth's forming are not. The sources called renewable and the source called nuclear draw on the same kind of energy. They differ in where the reaction runs and in how long its energy has waited.

**Definition 1.** The *origin* of a source is the process that first set its energy free.

**Theorem 1.** *The origin of sunlight, wind, falling water, wood, coal, oil and gas is fusion in the Sun.*

*Proof.* The Sun shines by fusing hydrogen into helium in its core, which the count of neutrinos arriving from it confirms. Sunlight warms the Earth unevenly and the differences in pressure that result drive the wind. Sunlight evaporates water and lifts it, so that it falls as rain on high ground and runs downhill. Plants hold sunlight in chemical bonds as they grow and wood is that holding. Coal, oil and gas are the buried remains of plants and plankton. $\square$

| Source | Path from the Sun | Delay |
|---|---|---|
| Sunlight | direct | 8 minutes |
| Wind | uneven heating of the air | days |
| Falling water | evaporation and rain | weeks to years |
| Wood | growth of plants | years to decades |
| Coal, oil and gas | growth and burial | millions of years |

**Corollary 1.** *Of the energy flowing through the surface of the Earth, the Sun supplies all but about three parts in ten thousand.*

The Earth intercepts about 173,000 terawatts of sunlight. Heat rising from within the Earth carries about 47 terawatts, of which roughly half is the decay of uranium, thorium and potassium and the rest is heat left from the Earth's forming. The tides, which draw on the turning of the Earth and the motion of the Moon, carry about 4.

**Corollary 2.** *Renewable names the size of a store and not a kind of source.*

Sunlight arrives on Earth as a flow and leaves the Sun as the release of a store that does not refill. The Sun holds hydrogen enough to fuse for about five billion years more. A source is called renewable when its store is so large that its end lies beyond concern. Coal is the same energy in a store that lasts centuries. Uranium is a store of heavy nuclei made in dying and colliding stars before the Earth formed.

**Corollary 3.** *The sources that are collected and the fuels that are burned differ by the delay alone.*

Coal is sunlight held for hundreds of millions of years and wind is sunlight held for days. The longer the delay the more of the energy sits in a store that can be drawn on at will and the less of it is renewed within a human span.

The claim concerns where the energy comes from. It says nothing of cost, of risk or of which source suits which need. It would be false if the wind or the cycle of water were driven mainly by something other than sunlight or if the Sun's output came from something other than fusion.

The wheel under the falling water is turned by a reaction 150 million kilometers away.
